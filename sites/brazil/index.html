LGPD · Brazil

Beaverless in Brazil

Brazil has no beavers. South America does have some, but they're unwelcome guests at the far southern tip of the continent.

Consent test page — LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados Pessoais). Brazilian template under the LGPD (Law 13.709/2018), enforced by the national data protection authority, ANPD.

Beaver facts

  • Brazil has no beavers, but it does have the capybara (capivara), the world's largest rodent. The beaver comes second.
  • South America's only beavers are invaders: in 1946 Argentina released twenty North American beavers in Tierra del Fuego to start a fur industry.
  • They have since multiplied to as many as 110,000 animals.
  • They have spread into Chile and onto the Argentine and Chilean mainlands, leaving dead forests and stagnant ponds behind them.
  • In 2008 Argentina and Chile agreed to eradicate them and restore the damaged ecosystems.
