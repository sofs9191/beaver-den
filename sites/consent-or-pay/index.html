Beaver facts

  • In the Hudson's Bay Company's fur trade, a made beaver — one prime, fully prepared pelt — was the unit of account. Kettles, blankets and guns were all priced in made beaver.
  • In the 1850s the company even struck brass tokens denominated in made beaver.
  • Beaver underfur has tiny barbs that lock together when pressed, which is why it made the densest, most waterproof felt for hats.
  • When silk top hats replaced beaver felt in the mid-1800s, demand for pelts collapsed, giving beaver populations room to recover.
